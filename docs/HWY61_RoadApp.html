The Problem

The day sheet is a PDF in a group text
that nobody can find.

The TM sends a day sheet every morning. It's a PDF attachment in the group thread — buried under 40 messages about where to get breakfast. By soundcheck, half the band is asking "what time is doors?" and the bus driver is asking "where's the hotel?" and the merch person is asking "what's the venue WiFi?"

The information exists. It's just never where anyone can find it when they need it.

One App

Open the app. See today's show. That's it.

The tour manager builds the tour in TourRouter. Every person on the road opens the Road App and sees exactly what they need for today — van call time, venue name and address, load-in, soundcheck, doors, showtime, curfew, hotel, drive time to the next city.

No account required. No cost. Enter a 6-character tour code from your TM and you're connected to the tour. The schedule updates in real time. When the TM changes a van call, everyone sees it immediately.

What You See

The info people on the road actually need.

01

Today's Show

The home screen is today. Van call, venue, load-in, soundcheck, doors, showtime, curfew. Everything you need at a glance. Open the app, get the answer.

02

Show Details

Tap any show for the full picture — production contacts, venue WiFi, parking instructions, venue notes, and any info the venue provided in the advance.

03

Tour Calendar

Every date on the tour. Shows, off days, travel days. Tap any date to see the details. Always know what's coming.

04

Travel Info

Drive time and distance to the next city. Hotel name, address, and confirmation number. Flight info when the leg is a fly date.

05

Role-Specific Views

FOH sees production notes and the input list link. Bus driver sees the route and parking. Merch manager sees show info plus a sales entry form. Everyone sees what's relevant to their job.

$̸

No Financial Data. Ever.

The Road App never shows offers, guarantees, settlements, deal terms, personnel pay, or commission information. That data doesn't exist in the app. It's not hidden behind a permission — it's excluded at the source.
