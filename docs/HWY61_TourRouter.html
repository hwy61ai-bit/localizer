The Problem

Touring runs on scattered systems
and manual math.

The routing lives in a spreadsheet. The budget lives in a different spreadsheet. The advance lives in an email chain. The settlement lives on a piece of paper at the merch table. The guest list lives in a text thread. Personnel pay lives in someone's head.

Every person on the tour is working from a different version of the truth. The tour manager reconciles it all — manually, every night, after the show, when they should be sleeping.

One wrong number in one cell cascades through everything. And nobody catches it until settlement day, when the promoter's math doesn't match yours and you're standing in a back office at midnight trying to figure out where $400 went.

This is how every touring act at the club-to-theater level operates. It's been this way for decades. Not because it works — because nothing better existed.

One System

TourRouter replaces the spreadsheets,
the email chains, and the napkin math.

Every person on the tour works from the same data. When a show is added, the budget updates. When an advance comes back, the show details populate. When a settlement is dropped in, the P&L shifts from projected to actual. When someone gets paid, the finance layer reflects it.

Nothing is re-entered. Nothing is copy-pasted. Nothing lives in someone's inbox. The tour manager's job goes from "reconcile everything manually" to "review and confirm."

What It Does

Nine systems. One platform.

01

Routing & Budget

Add shows manually or drop a route sheet. Real drive times and distances between every city. Gas costs based on your vehicle. Long drives flagged. Drive-vs-fly toggle on every leg. The budget builds itself — guarantees, expenses, per diems, hotels, personnel, commissions — a live P&L that updates with every change.

02

Document Intake

Drop any touring document — settlement sheet, deal memo, hotel confirmation, receipt, advance response — as a PDF, photo, scan, or Excel file. The system reads it, figures out what it is, matches it to the right show, and stages every number for review. Handwritten settlements, blurry scans, crossed-out numbers — all handled.

03

Settlement

Drop the settlement sheet in after the show. TourRouter reads every number — ticket gross, expenses, deductions, artist payment. Matches it to the right show. You review, confirm, and the show moves from projected to actual. All 14 deal types supported. Promoter math verification automatic. Night-of settlement in minutes.

04

Advancing

Advance emails on your schedule — first email, first follow-up, second follow-up, final nudge. Venues fill in a web form with load-in, WiFi, parking, contacts, and notes. Venue goes dark close to show day? Automatic escalation. Pause any show. Turn it off entirely. Always in control.

05

Personnel & Pay

Day rate, off-day rate, weekly rate, per-show rate, or percentage of net. TourRouter calculates what each person is owed based on the actual schedule. Band on splits, crew on day rates, hybrid structures — all supported. Numbers feed directly into the tour P&L.

06

Finance Layer

Income waterfall, per-show P&L, commission breakdown, net-to-artist — projected and actual side by side. When every show is settled, generate an end-of-tour report as a PDF and share it with anyone via link. No login required for the recipient.

07

Guest List

Per-show lists with allotments by pass type — Guest, Industry, Press, Photo. Anyone on the tour adds names to their allotment. TM sets cutoff times. Show day: generate a door list PDF to hand to the venue.

08

Commissions

Commission structures for agents, managers, anyone who takes a cut. Percentage of gross, net, or custom. The waterfall shows exactly how money flows from gross income to net-to-artist. You decide which roles see the breakdown.

09

Multi-Currency

Every show in its own currency — USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, and more. Everything converts to your base currency for the tour P&L using daily exchange rates. No manual conversion. No rounding errors.
