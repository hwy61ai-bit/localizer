# Localizer Onboarding Walkthrough — Draft Script

**Target length:** ~2:30
**Tone:** Direct, conversational. Tour-manager-to-tour-manager.
**Status:** First-pass draft. Loose. Edit hard to voice before recording.

---

## [00:00 — Hook] Screen: hwy61labs.com landing page or Localizer dashboard

If you've ever announced a tour, you know the routine. You spend a half-day
in Photoshop making the same flyer twenty different ways — Instagram post,
Instagram story, Facebook cover, TikTok vertical, YouTube short — and then
you email those off to every venue, one by one.

Localizer fixes that.

## [00:20 — Artist] Screen: artists page, click "+ New artist", type a name

You start with your artist. Just the name to get going. You can add your
logo, band photo, and brand colors later when you're ready.

## [00:30 — Tour] Screen: create a tour, name it (e.g., "Summer 2026")

Then a tour. Five-show run, forty-date album cycle — same workflow either way.

## [00:40 — Schedule import] Screen: drag-drop a CSV or PDF schedule into the import zone

Here's where it gets fun. Drop in your schedule — CSV from your booking
agent, PDF deal memo, an Excel from your tour manager. Localizer reads it.
Venues, dates, cities — it pulls everything out and stages it for you to
review before anything saves.

[Pause to let viewer see the imported schedule]

You review it, you confirm it, and your tour is built.

## [01:10 — Asset upload] Screen: assets page, upload landscape + square + vertical images

Now upload your tour art. One landscape, one square, one vertical — that's
the source set. Localizer generates every aspect ratio every platform
expects from those three files. Sponsor logos and band logos are optional
overlays if you want them.

## [01:30 — Template] Screen: open template editor, drag text positions, pick a font

Drag your venue name, date, and city wherever they look right on the layout.
Pick a font, pick a text color. That's your template — and it applies to
every show on the tour automatically.

## [01:50 — Generate] Screen: click "Generate All", show progress bar

One click. Localizer renders every format for every show — Instagram posts,
stories, Facebook covers, TikTok and Reels, YouTube Shorts. Print PDFs too
if you've got promoters who still want a printable.

## [02:10 — Venue link] Screen: copy a venue link, then load it in a second window to show what venues see

Every show on your tour gets its own venue link. Send that single link to
the venue or promoter, and they download exactly what they need in exactly
the format they want. No more "hey can you resend this as a story" emails
at 11pm.

## [02:35 — Close] Screen: back to dashboard

That's Localizer. Get back the day you used to spend exporting flyers.

When you're ready, start with your first artist.

## Editing notes

- **The 11pm story-resend line** — specific moment everyone recognizes. Worth keeping; it lands.
- **"Fixes that" softened from "kills that workflow"** — push back harder if you want more attitude.
- **No mention of pricing, tiers, or trial** — this is a product walkthrough, not a sales video. Pricing belongs on the pricing page.
- **No competitor framing** — leave that for sales conversations.
- **The "you can add later" lines** are doing real work. They lower activation barrier and tell viewers "don't get stuck on this step." Keep.
