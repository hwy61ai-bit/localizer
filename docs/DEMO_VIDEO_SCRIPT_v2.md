# Localizer Onboarding Walkthrough — Draft v2

Target length: ~2:55 • Tone: direct, conversational, tour-manager-to-tour-manager.
Status: second-pass draft, Drew-approved structure. Edit hard to voice before recording.

---

## [00:00 — Hook]
Screen: localizer.music landing page or Localizer dashboard

If you've ever announced a tour, you know the routine. You spend a half-day in Photoshop making the same flyer twenty different ways — Instagram post, Instagram story, Facebook cover, TikTok vertical, YouTube short — and then you email those off to every venue, one by one.

Localizer fixes that.

## [00:20 — Artist]
Screen: artists page, click "+ New artist", type a name

You start with your artist. Just the name to get going. You can add your logo, band photo, and brand colors later when you're ready.

## [00:30 — Tour]
Screen: create a tour, name it (e.g., "Summer 2026")

Then a tour. Five-show run, forty-date album cycle — same workflow either way.

## [00:40 — Schedule import]
Screen: drag-drop a CSV or PDF schedule into the import zone

Here's where it gets fun. Drop in your schedule — CSV from your booking agent, PDF deal memo, an Excel from your tour manager. Localizer reads it. Venues, dates, cities — it pulls everything out and stages it for you to review before anything saves.

[Pause to let viewer see the imported schedule]

You review it, you confirm it, and your tour is built.

## [01:10 — Asset upload]
Screen: assets page, upload landscape + square + vertical images

Now upload your tour art. One landscape, one square, one vertical — that's the source set. Localizer generates every aspect ratio every platform expects from those three files. Sponsor logos and band logos are optional overlays if you want them.

## [01:30 — Template]
Screen: open template editor, drag text positions, pick a font

Drag your venue name, date, and city wherever they look right on the layout. Pick a font, pick a text color. That's your template — and it applies to every show on the tour automatically.

## [01:50 — Generate]
Screen: click "Generate All", show progress bar

One click. Localizer renders every format for every show — Instagram posts, stories, Facebook covers, TikTok and Reels, YouTube Shorts. Print PDFs too if you've got promoters who still want a printable.

## [02:10 — Venue link]
Screen: copy a venue link, then load it in a second window; scroll the page top to bottom at a steady pace — Team section visible only briefly in the scroll

Every show on your tour gets its own venue link. Send that single link, and the venue gets everything they need on one page — the assets in every format, your bio, your press and playlists, your socials. They download exactly what they want, in exactly the format they want. No more "hey can you resend this as a story" emails at 11pm.

## [02:35 — Share & Download]
Screen: back on the tour page, click "SHARE & DOWNLOAD", show the modal, copy the marketing link

And when it's time to loop in your team — your publicist, your digital person, whoever's actually posting this stuff — one button shares the whole tour. Every show, every asset, one link. Or grab it all as a download yourself. Either way, nobody's digging through email threads for the right file.

## [02:55 — Close]
Screen: back to dashboard

That's Localizer. Get back the day you used to spend exporting flyers.

When you're ready, start with your first artist.
