# Localizer Beta — User Guide

A short guide for new beta users. Send this (or a version of it) along with the invite code.

---

## Getting started with the Localizer beta

Welcome! Here's everything you need to get in.

**1. Go to the login page:** https://www.hwy61labs.com/login

**2. Enter your invite code** (the one we sent you — looks like `HWY61-BETA-XXX`) and click VERIFY.

**3. Enter your email address** and click SEND LOGIN LINK.

**4. Check your email** for a message from HWY61 Labs. Click the sign-in link in that email.

**5. You're in.** You'll land on a welcome screen — click GET STARTED to jump to your dashboard and add your first artist.

**Coming back later?** Just go to https://www.hwy61labs.com/login and click the **SIGN IN** link at the bottom (under "Already have an account?"). No need to enter your invite code again — one-time use.

### A few notes

- Magic links expire in 1 hour. If yours does, just request a new one.
- Open the magic link in the same browser where you requested it. (If it opens in a different browser, it won't work — copy the link URL and paste it into the original window instead.)
- Questions, bugs, or feedback? Just reply to the welcome email.
