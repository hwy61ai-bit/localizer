## 1. Pricing

Happy to break it down. Localizer comes in these paid tiers, all on a 14-day free trial:

- **Indie** — $19/mo, 1 artist
- **Pro** — $39/mo, up to 5 artists
- **Agency** — $199/mo, up to 12 artists
- **Agency Pro** — contact sales for custom pricing

Annual billing is two months free on any tier. You can switch tiers or cancel any time from Account Settings — no calls, no back-and-forth.

If you're not sure which fits, tell me how many artists you're routing and I'll point you to the right one.

---

## 2. Billing

Thanks for reaching out. You can see your current plan, payment method, invoices, and next renewal date under Account Settings → Billing. Changes to your card or plan take effect there immediately. If something looks off — a charge you didn't expect, a renewal date that's wrong — reply here with the details and I'll dig in. Billing questions come straight to me.

---

## 3. "How do I do X" (general how-to)

Great question. The short version of getting an artist's tour out the door:

1. Add your artist
2. Drop in your schedule (CSV, PDF deal memo, or Excel — Localizer reads all of it)
3. Upload your tour art plus your W-9, stage plot, and FOH docs
4. Click Generate — every show gets its own share link with every asset and doc inside

If you tell me the specific step you're stuck on, I can walk you through it. Our help docs cover the common ones too: [help docs link].

---

## 4. Refund requests

Thanks for letting me know, and sorry Localizer wasn't the right fit. We don't process refunds — the 14-day free trial is there so you get a full run at the product before you're ever charged. If you cancel from Account Settings you won't be billed again, and your access runs through the end of the current period.

---

## 5. "Is my data safe"

Good question to ask. Your tour data and assets are stored on managed infrastructure with per-account access controls, so other customers can't see your data. The venue share links use long, randomly generated tokens, so a link only works for whoever you send it to. You can delete an artist, tour, or asset any time, and you own everything you upload.

If you've got a specific concern, reply here and I'll answer it directly.
