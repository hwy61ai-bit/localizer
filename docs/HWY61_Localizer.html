The Problem

Tour marketing is a production bottleneck
disguised as a creative task.

An artist announces a 20-date tour. That means 20 Instagram stories, 20 Facebook event images, 20 X posts, 20 posters for the venues, 20 web graphics. Each platform has different dimensions. Each venue has a different logo to include. Each promoter needs files in a different format.

So someone on the team opens Photoshop or Canva and starts duplicating files. Changing dates. Changing city names. Changing venue names. Resizing for every platform. Exporting. Uploading. Emailing individual files to individual promoters who email back asking for a different size.

What It Does

Upload. Generate. Distribute.

01

Automatic Asset Generation

One promo image becomes a complete set of show assets. Instagram Story (1080×1920), Facebook Event (1920×1005), X/Twitter Post (1200×675), Poster (11×17), Web Graphic (1200×630) — every format, every show, every time. Add a custom format and it generates retroactively across all shows.

02

Brand Control

Upload your fonts. Set your colors. Choose your layout. Every asset is on-brand without manual design work. Consistent across every show, every platform, every market.

03

Promoter Distribution

Generate a single shareable link for each tour. Promoters click the link and see every asset for their show, pre-sized for every platform. They download what they need. No back-and-forth. No "can you send me the Instagram version?"

04

Venue-Branded Assets

Promoters and venues can use Localizer from their side too — pull artist promo images and generate venue-branded versions for their own marketing. Same automation, their branding.

05

Real-Time Updates

Change a date, add a show, swap the promo image — every asset across every platform updates automatically. No re-exporting. No re-sending.

06

Multi-Artist Support

Agencies and management companies with multiple acts manage all their tour marketing from one dashboard. Each artist has their own brand settings. Assets generate per-artist across all tours.
