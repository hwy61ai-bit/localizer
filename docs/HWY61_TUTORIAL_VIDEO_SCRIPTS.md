# HWY61 — Tutorial Video Scripts
**April 2026**
**Format:** 1–3 minutes each. Tim records screen + voiceover.
**Tone:** Casual, confident, like you're showing a friend. Not a software demo — a "here's how I do this" walkthrough.
**Rule:** No mention of AI. It's "HWY61 reads it" / "HWY61 figures it out" / "the system matches it."

## Video 6: Managing the Advance Pipeline
**Length:** ~2 minutes
**Start screen:** Tour with shows in various advance states

---

**[Show: Tour page or advance pipeline view]**

Advancing — the part of touring nobody loves but everybody needs. HWY61 handles it for you.

**[Point out advance status badges on different shows]**

Look at the status on each show. This one says "Confirmed" — the venue responded, all the info is in. This one says "Sent" — we emailed the venue, waiting to hear back. This one says "Follow-up 1 Sent" — they didn't respond, so HWY61 sent a follow-up automatically.

**[Click into a show that's been advanced]**

Let me click into this confirmed show. You can see everything the venue sent back — load-in time, soundcheck, WiFi, parking, production contact, settlement contact. All of this came in through the advance form we sent them.

**[Show the advance form link or email]**

Here's how it works. HWY61 sends the venue an email with a link to a form. The venue fills it out — load-in, contacts, WiFi, whatever you ask for. When they submit, it lands right here on the show record. No back-and-forth emails. No "hey, can you resend that info?"

**[Point out escalation]**

And this show here — see "Escalated"? That means HWY61 sent the initial email, two follow-ups, and a final nudge, and the venue still hasn't responded. Now it's flagged for you to pick up the phone. The system did the chasing — you handle the ones that actually need a human.

**[End screen: Pipeline view]**

Set it up once at the start of the tour, and every show gets advanced on schedule without you thinking about it.

## Video 7: Reading the Finance Dashboard
**Length:** ~2 minutes
**Start screen:** Tour financials page

---

**[Show: Finance dashboard]**

This is where the money lives. Everything flows into one view — no spreadsheet, no calculator, no formulas you built yourself.

**[Point out stat cards at the top]**

Up top — the headlines. Gross income, total expenses, net to artist. These are your projected numbers based on the offers and rates you've entered. As shows settle, you'll see actual numbers appear right next to them.

**[Scroll to income breakdown]**

Here's the income waterfall. Guarantees across all shows, then backend and merch projections if you've entered those. Then commissions — see the breakdown? Agent at 10%, manager at 15%. HWY61 calculates the waterfall so you see exactly what flows through to the artist.

**[Point out per-show P&L]**

Scroll down and there's the per-show breakdown. Every show with its guarantee, expenses, and net. Green means you're ahead of projection, red means you're behind. These three shows here are settled — those are real numbers. Everything else is still projected.

**[Toggle: Projected vs. Actual]**

This toggle switches between projected and actual. As more shows settle, the actual column fills in. By the end of the tour, everything is actual and you can close the books.

**[Show: Export / end-of-tour report]**

When you're ready, hit "Generate Report" and HWY61 builds an end-of-tour financial report. PDF, Excel, or both. You can share it with a link — the artist or manager clicks the link and sees the report, no login needed.

**[End screen: Report preview]**

That's the whole financial picture of the tour in one place. No spreadsheet ever.

## Video 8: Setting Up the Master Artist Profile
**Length:** ~1.5 minutes
**Start screen:** Dashboard, click gear icon on an artist tile

---

**[Show: Dashboard with artist tile, click gear icon]**

The Master Artist Profile is where you set the defaults for everything — so you don't re-enter the same info every time you create a tour.

**[Show: Artist profile page]**

At the top, artist name and logo. I'll upload a PNG here — transparent background works best.

**[Scroll to roster section]**

Here's the default roster. These are the people who go on every tour. I'll add my TM, FOH engineer, monitor engineer, guitar tech, merch person, and the band members. I can set day rates and off-day rates for each person right here.

**[Point out the inheritance behavior]**

Here's the key — when I create a new tour for this artist, the roster copies over automatically. So I'm not rebuilding it from scratch every time. If I need to swap someone out for a specific tour, I edit it at the tour level and it doesn't change the default.

**[Scroll to commissions section if visible]**

Same thing with commissions. Set your agent's percentage and your manager's percentage here, and they'll carry over to every new tour.

**[End screen: Completed profile]**

Set this up once, and every new tour starts with the right people and the right numbers already in place. Two minutes of setup saves you hours down the road.
