## Problem

**Section tag:** THE PROBLEM

**Headline:** Touring runs on scattered systems and manual math.

The routing lives in a spreadsheet. The budget lives in a different spreadsheet. The advance lives in an email chain. The settlement lives on a piece of paper at the merch table. The guest list lives in a text thread. Personnel pay lives in someone's head.

Every person on the tour is working from a different version of the truth. The tour manager reconciles it all — manually, every night, after the show, when they should be sleeping.

One wrong number in one cell cascades through everything. And nobody catches it until settlement day, when the promoter's math doesn't match yours and you're standing in a back office at midnight trying to figure out where $400 went.

This is how every touring act at the club-to-theater level operates. It's been this way for decades. Not because it works — because nothing better existed.

---

## Solution

**Section tag:** ONE SYSTEM

**Headline:** TourRouter replaces the spreadsheets, the email chains, and the napkin math.

Every person on the tour works from the same data. When a show is added, the budget updates. When an advance comes back, the show details populate. When a settlement is dropped in, the P&L shifts from projected to actual. When someone gets paid, the finance layer reflects it.

Nothing is re-entered. Nothing is copy-pasted. Nothing lives in someone's inbox. The tour manager's job goes from "reconcile everything manually" to "review and confirm."

---

## Features

**Section tag:** WHAT IT DOES

### Routing & Budget
Add shows manually or drop a route sheet. TourRouter calculates real drive times and distances between every city. Gas costs estimate automatically based on your vehicle. Long drives get flagged. Every leg has a drive-vs-fly toggle that shows you the cost of each option. The budget builds itself as you route — guarantees, expenses, per diems, hotels, personnel, commissions — all flowing into a live P&L that updates with every change.

### Universal Document Intake
Drop any touring document into TourRouter — settlement sheet, deal memo, hotel confirmation, receipt, advance response — as a PDF, photo, scan, or Excel file. The system reads it, figures out what kind of document it is, matches it to the right show, and stages every number for your review. You confirm before anything saves. Photos of handwritten settlements work. Blurry scans work. Crossed-out numbers with corrections work.

### Settlement
After a show, drop the settlement sheet in. TourRouter reads every number — ticket gross, expenses, deductions, artist payment — and matches it to the right show. You review, confirm, and the show moves from projected to actual in your tour P&L. All 14 standard deal types are supported, and the system verifies that the promoter's math is correct. Night-of settlement in minutes.

### Advancing
Set a schedule and TourRouter sends advance emails to your venue contacts automatically. First email, first follow-up, second follow-up, final nudge — all on your timeline. Venues can fill in a web form with load-in times, WiFi, parking, production contacts, and notes. If a venue goes dark close to show day, TourRouter escalates to the tour manager. Pause it on any show. Turn it off entirely. You're always in control.

### Personnel & Pay
Add everyone on the tour to the roster — day rate, off-day rate, weekly rate, per-show rate, or percentage of net. TourRouter calculates what each person is owed based on the actual schedule. Band members on splits, crew on day rates, hybrid structures — all supported. The numbers feed directly into the tour P&L.

### Finance Layer
A read-only dashboard that pulls everything together. Income waterfall, per-show P&L, commission breakdown, net-to-artist — projected and actual side by side. When every show is settled, generate an end-of-tour report as a PDF and share it with anyone via link. No login required for the recipient.

### Guest List
Per-show guest lists with allotments by pass type — Guest, Industry, Press, Photo. Anyone on the tour adds names to their allotment. The TM sets cutoff times. On show day, generate a door list PDF to hand to the venue.

### Commissions
Set up commission structures for agents, managers, or anyone who takes a cut. Percentage of gross, percentage of net, custom structures. The waterfall shows exactly how money flows from gross income to net-to-artist. You decide which roles see the commission breakdown.

### Multi-Currency
Every show can be in a different currency — USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, and more. The system converts everything to your base currency for the tour P&L using daily exchange rates. No manual conversion. No rounding errors.
